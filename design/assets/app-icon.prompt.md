# 小程序图标

使用内置 image_gen 生成，再通过同一工具编辑为完整底色版本。最终文件：`app-icon.png`。保留工具输出原图，未手工修图。

## 初始提示词

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square WeChat mini program app icon for a family shared to-do and reminders app.
Primary request: Create one polished, original app icon combining a welcoming home and a clear completion checkmark into a single memorable symbol.
Subject: A bold soft ivory house silhouette, with rounded corners and a gently peaked roof. A large blue-violet checkmark is cut into its center as clean negative space. The home conveys family care, the check conveys getting daily things done together.
Style: exceptionally clean flat vector-like brand mark, warm, calm, friendly, balanced geometric curves, refined optical alignment, high contrast and legible at small sizes.
Color palette: use the project's brand blue-violet #5860B5 as a solid full-bleed background and warm ivory #FFF9F1 for the main house symbol.
Composition: square 1024 by 1024 image, one centered symbol occupying about 62 percent of the canvas, generous even padding. Solid background extends to all four edges, no pre-rounded outer app tile and no surrounding frame.
Constraints: no words, no letters, no numbers, no watermarks, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no fine details, no separate notification badges, no extra icons. Deliver the actual app icon artwork only.

## 最终编辑提示词

Use case: precise-object-edit
Edit target: the supplied house-and-check app icon.
Keep the same ivory house silhouette with rounded edges and the blue-violet checkmark.
Change the entire canvas background to a uniform fully opaque blue-violet #5860B5 extending to every edge of the square. All background pixels must be opaque and solid, no black background, no transparency, no checkerboard. Remove any rough fringe around the house, making its edge clean and smooth against the blue-violet background.
The house and check should be a simple flat two-color graphic, no texture, no shadow or gradient.
Recenter the house symbol with generous even padding so it occupies around 62% of the canvas width.
Output a square complete app icon, not a cutout/sticker. No text, no border, no mockup.
